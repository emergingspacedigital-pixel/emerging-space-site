Last updated: 5 November 2026

Emerging Space Digital Marketing (“we”, “us”, “our”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect when you visit emergingspace.in, fill in one of our forms (including forms on Facebook and Instagram), or contact us, and how we use and protect it.

1. Information we collect

2. How we use your information

3. How we share your information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers that help us run our business, such as Meta (Facebook, Instagram and WhatsApp), Google, and our website hosting provider, and only as needed to provide our services. 4. Cookies and advertising

Our website may use the Meta Pixel to understand how visitors use our site and to show relevant ads on Facebook and Instagram. You can manage cookies in your browser settings, and you can control the ads you see through your Facebook and Instagram ad preferences.

5. How long we keep your information

We keep your information only for as long as needed for the purposes above, or as required by law. If you don’t become a client, we delete enquiry details when they’re no longer needed.

6. How we protect your information

We take reasonable steps to keep your information secure and to limit access to people who need it to do their work. No method of storing or sending data online is completely secure, but we work to protect your information.

7. Your choices and rights

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete your personal information, or withdraw your consent to being contacted, at any time. We handle your information in line with applicable Indian law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. To make a request, contact us using the details below.

8. Children

Our services are meant for businesses and adults. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.
