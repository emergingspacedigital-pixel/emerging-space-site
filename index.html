Questions founders ask us

Is the ad budget included in the price?

No. Our fee covers strategy, creatives and management. Your ad budget is paid directly to Meta or Google, and we recommend starting with at least ₹300–500 a day.

Do you guarantee sales?

No honest agency can guarantee sales. What we do guarantee is a clear plan, constant testing and a transparent report every week.

How fast can we start?

Usually within 2–3 days of your strategy call, once we have access to your page and ad account.

I’m a dropshipper. Can you help?

Yes. We work with Shopify and other store builders, and can help you test products with ads before you scale.

Who owns my ad account and page?

You do. We work inside your accounts with partner access, so everything stays yours.
